## Qualification boundary

This canary proves that the selected F2FS/fscrypt changes retain the essential
TWRP and Android encrypted-userdata contract on the tested CPH2747.  Full
release qualification still requires a second clean boot and interactive
coverage such as direct cellular-data isolation, Bluetooth HID/audio, NFC,
camera, audio, fingerprint, charging/USB, suspend/resume, and deep idle.

Raw recovery, dmesg, and logcat evidence remains in the local verified test
backup directory and is intentionally not committed.

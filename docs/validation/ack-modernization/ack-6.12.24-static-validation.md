## Physical test plan

The later authorized physical test must use the existing hardened TWRP helper,
verify the immutable 6.12.23 rollback payloads, dry-run first, and write only
the matching 6.12.24 `system_dlkm` and `boot` payloads. The retained
`vendor_dlkm`, stock `vendor_boot`, VBMeta, and slot metadata must not be
rewritten. Write `system_dlkm` first and `boot` last; read back and hash both
partitions before Android boot.

Acceptance requires two clean boots reporting the exact 6.12.24 release; the
46-entry/wwan-21 module contract; zero symbol, CRC, vermagic, signature, or
protected-export errors; 6 GHz WPA3 and Wi-Fi reload; cellular HOME/IN_SERVICE,
dual-stack RMNET, routes, IP/DNS, and Wi-Fi handoff; speaker/media/ringtone;
camera open/capture; Bluetooth toggle/reconnect; NFC toggle and Wallet/HCE;
UI/GPU sanity; USB enumeration; deep idle/resume; and a full panic, Oops, BUG,
KASAN, UBSAN, IOMMU/SMMU, and hung-task scan.

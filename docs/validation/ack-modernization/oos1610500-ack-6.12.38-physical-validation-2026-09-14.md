## Preflight and write safety

The phone began in healthy Android on the physically qualified ACK 6.12.37
kernel.  `sys.boot_completed=1`, user 0 was `RUNNING_UNLOCKED`, and `/data`
was mounted read-write as F2FS with `inlinecrypt`.

TWRP 3.7.1_16 reported project 24863, model CPH2747, product CPH2747NA,
product board `canoe`, Qualcomm SM8850, and slot `_a`.  Its installed helper
still expected the narrower recovery aliases `ro.product.name=CPH2747` and
`ro.board.platform=canoe`; a temporary RAM-only helper copy accepted the
observed CPH2747NA alias and required the combined SM8850 plus canoe evidence.
The remaining device, slot, snapshot, magic, alignment, capacity, backup, and
read-back checks were unchanged.  The dry run passed before the write.

The complete 100,663,296-byte `boot_a` partition was backed up twice:

- host: `boot_a-before.img` in the evidence directory;
- recovery: `/sdcard/TWRP/kernel-flash-backups/controlled-stack-a-20260914-214441/boot_a.img`.

Both backups have the qualified parent SHA-256.  Only `boot_a` was written.
The helper read-back, independent recovery block hash, and independent full
host read-back all matched the candidate SHA-256 exactly.  Active-slot
metadata was not changed.

Automated runtime checks passed:

- WLAN connected to the WPA3-SAE network at 6135 MHz; direct traffic had
  zero packet loss on both boots.
- `rmnet_data2` had IPv4 and IPv6 addresses; direct cellular IPv4 and DNS
  traffic had zero packet loss.
- Bluetooth initialized `ON`; the existing paired-device records and active
  link state were present.
- NFC initialized `on`.
- ZRAM was active at 8,388,604 KiB and CPUFreq reported the WALT governor.
- 76 APEX mounts and 47 loop devices were present.
- Camera services enumerated five camera devices, fingerprint reported zero
  HAL deaths, and audio/camera/NFC services were running.
- USB ADB remained operational and USB power was detected.
- 665 modules were loaded; the loaded module-name set exactly matched the
  qualified ACK 6.12.37 second-boot set.

The two kernel and framework scans found no unknown symbols, version/CRC
disagreements, invalid module formats, bad vermagic, signature failures,
protected-export failures, panics, oopses, Rescue Party, user0 failure, or
APEX mount failure.  The missing Oplus OTA cleanup-file message also exists on
the qualified ACK 6.12.37 baseline and is not a new candidate regression.

## Qualification boundary

After the controlled test, the device owner reported that the kernel "works
great" during normal use.  This confirms continued successful operation after
the automated canary, but individual manual peripheral results were not
reported separately.

This result is sufficient for a boot-qualified canary.  It does not claim a
new manual end-to-end test of Bluetooth HID/audio, NFC Wallet, camera capture,
audio playback/recording, fingerprint authentication, or five unplugged deep
idle cycles.  Those checks remain required before calling ACK 6.12.38 fully
physically qualified or promoting it to `main`.

The tested 6.12.38 kernel remains installed on `boot_a`; the qualified 6.12.37
rollback is retained both on the host and in decrypted TWRP storage.

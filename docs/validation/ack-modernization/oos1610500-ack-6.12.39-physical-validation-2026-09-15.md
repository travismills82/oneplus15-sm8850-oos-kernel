## Preflight, write, and rollback integrity

The phone started from healthy ACK 6.12.38 on OxygenOS 16.0.10.500 with
`sys.boot_completed=1`, user 0 `RUNNING_UNLOCKED`, and `/data` mounted as
read-write F2FS with `inlinecrypt`. TWRP identified the device as CPH2747,
SM8850/Canoe, slot `_a`, and reported a 100,663,296-byte boot partition.

Before each write, the entire target boot partition was backed up and its
device and host hashes were compared. Only `boot_a` was written. The candidate
write, diagnostic rollback, and owner-requested candidate restore all passed
device-side and independent host-side full-partition read-back comparison.

No command wrote `system_dlkm`, `vendor_dlkm`, `vendor_boot`, DTBO, VBMeta,
metadata, userdata, or slot metadata. The exact stock supporting payload
contract remained installed.

## Known DRM warning

ACK 6.12.39 emits a new repeated warning absent from the qualified ACK 6.12.38
run:

```text
WARNING: ... drm_gem_object_handle_put_unlocked
drm_gem_fb_destroy
drm_framebuffer_free
drm_mode_object_put
msm_ioctl_rmfb2 [msm_drm]
```

Five warning headers occurred on the first candidate boot and 26 on the second.
The path intersects the upstream ACK 6.12.39 GEM handle-lifetime change
`5f74664396ca01785f3ae13e292960ae4516cdd2`. Android's subsequent KABI revert
`4a5ce5aeeca24b349f541116f022e80532bcad76`, carried locally as
`91380ffbe5a1`, removes the ABI-breaking generic framebuffer implementation but
does not remove the warning on the Qualcomm `msm_ioctl_rmfb2` path.

The warning produced no observed display failure, panic, pstore record, Android
boot failure, FBE failure, or owner-observed normal-use failure. It remains a
documented compatibility issue for follow-up rather than being hidden from the
physical record.

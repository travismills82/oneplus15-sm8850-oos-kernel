# OOS 16.0.10.500 ACK 6.12.25 FBE and storage audit

## Result

**PASS — no change to the qualified 6.12.24 FBE, metadata-encryption, key, or
inline-crypto contract.** Existing encrypted user0 still requires physical
validation; this is a static result only.

The generated candidate configuration is semantically identical to the
physically qualified 6.12.24 configuration. It retains:

```text
CONFIG_FS_ENCRYPTION=y
CONFIG_FS_ENCRYPTION_INLINE_CRYPT=y
CONFIG_BLK_INLINE_ENCRYPTION=y
CONFIG_DM_DEFAULT_KEY=y
CONFIG_DM_CRYPT=y
CONFIG_KEYS=y
CONFIG_SCSI_UFS_CRYPTO=y
CONFIG_SECURITY_SELINUX=y
```

`CONFIG_ENCRYPTED_KEYS`, `CONFIG_TRUSTED_KEYS`, and
`CONFIG_KEY_DH_OPERATIONS` remain disabled exactly as in the qualified parent.
Separate `CONFIG_F2FS_FS_ENCRYPTION` and `CONFIG_EXT4_FS_ENCRYPTION` symbols
are absent in both generated configurations.

## Direct source review

The effective 6.12.24 to 6.12.25 source delta is empty under:

- `fs/crypto/` and fscrypt interfaces;
- `block/blk-crypto*` and inline-crypto interfaces;
- `drivers/md/dm-default-key*` and `drivers/md/dm-crypt*`;
- `security/keys/` and keyring interfaces;
- F2FS and EXT4 source;
- Qualcomm UFS, ICE, and inline-key consumers;
- Android storage vendor-hook definitions.

Storage-adjacent changes are limited to generic SCSI drivers and
`drivers/ufs/host/ufs-exynos.c`. The affected FNIC, HiSilicon SAS, MegaRAID,
MPI3MR, MPT3SAS, PM8001, QLogic, SmartPQI, and Exynos UFS drivers are absent or
disabled in the Canoe configuration. None is the Qualcomm UFS/ICE path used by
existing user0 initialization.

## Supporting invariants

- Semantic config delta: 0.
- `Module.symvers` is byte-identical to qualified 6.12.24.
- All five ABI reports are empty; the KMI symbol-list target passes.
- All 1,020 exact stock modules pass 57,216 import/CRC edges.
- No system_dlkm, vendor_dlkm, vendor_boot, DT, VBMeta, slot metadata,
  userdata, or metadata image was built or modified.

The physical gate remains decisive: boot only the new Image against the exact
stock payloads and require existing user0 to reach `RUNNING_UNLOCKED` with no
`init_user0_failed`. Formatting data or weakening encryption/policy is not an
acceptable fallback.

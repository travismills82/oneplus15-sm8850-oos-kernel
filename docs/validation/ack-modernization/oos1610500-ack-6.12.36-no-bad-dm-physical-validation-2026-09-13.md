## Result and rollback state

The complete ACK 6.12.36 candidate without the two bad DM patches passes the
boot-only existing-userdata/TWRP FBE canary and two clean Android boots.  This
demonstrates that the previously isolated DM patches are not required for the
6.12.36 point-release transition and that their exclusion restores the tested
encrypted-storage contract.

The passing candidate remains installed on `boot_a`.  Rollback was not needed;
the exact pre-test backup remains available and hash-verified.  No push, merge,
tag, release, or full physical qualification is claimed.

## Kernel, module, and framework scan

The final fatal scan found no use-after-free, KASAN/UBSAN report, kernel BUG,
Oops, panic, refcount failure, double free, invalid pointer, list corruption,
RCU stall, unresolved symbol, CRC/version disagreement, signature failure,
`init_user0_failed`, or critical framework crash loop.

Boot-time warning signatures were compared with the physically qualified B08
logs. The ACK run contained no new warning class; its observed Oplus touch-HBP
`frame_put()` warning was already present in B08, while several early B08 OEM
warning classes did not recur. The two init-subcontext SIGABRT records in the
Android crash buffer also match the B08 baseline and did not affect Android,
user0, or framework health.

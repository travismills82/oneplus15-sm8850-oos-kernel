## Kernel, module, and framework scan

The final scans found no use-after-free, KASAN/UBSAN report, kernel BUG, Oops,
panic, refcount failure, list corruption, RCU stall, unresolved symbol,
CRC/version disagreement, invalid module format, signature failure,
protected-export failure, `init_user0_failed`, modem restart, or critical
framework crash loop.

An early transient child process named `init` emitted `SIGABRT` records on
both test boots.  The retained pre-candidate crash ring from the qualified
6.12.25 state contains the same signature.  Android, system_server, FBE,
radios, and user0 initialization were unaffected, and no pstore record was
created.  This is classified as a pre-existing rooted/Oplus boot warning, not
a new 6.12.26 regression.

The downstream FBE cleanup warning for
`/data/oplus_ota_package/.recovery_repair` and existing Oplus debug/watchdog
messages remain non-fatal.  No new point-release-specific fatal warning class
was observed.

Complete first- and second-boot `dmesg`, `logcat`, and `/proc/modules`
captures are retained below the ignored backup directory named above.  Their
hashes are recorded with the local evidence and are not committed.

## Decision

**PASS — OOS 16.0.10.500 CUSTOM ACK 6.12.26 WITH EXACT STOCK DLKMS IS
PHYSICALLY QUALIFIED**

The exact tested source may be frozen as
`feature/oos1610500-ack-6.12.26-qualified` and tagged
`oos16.0.10.500-ack-6.12.26-qualified`.  `main` must remain unchanged unless
separately authorized, and ACK 6.12.27 must not begin as part of this
single-subversion experiment.

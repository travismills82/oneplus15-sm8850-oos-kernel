## Kernel, module, and framework scan

The final post-fingerprint scan found no use-after-free, KASAN/UBSAN report,
kernel BUG, Oops, panic, refcount failure, list corruption, RCU stall,
unresolved symbol, CRC/version disagreement, invalid module format, signature
failure, protected-export failure, `init_user0_failed`, modem restart, or
critical framework crash loop.

The exact stock module-name set is unchanged from qualified 6.12.26. The
static audit remains clean across all 1,020 stock modules, including 57,216
import/CRC edges and all 166 `rust_binder.ko` imports.

The Oplus `oplus-netd.o` `skfilter_egress_sysdrop` BPF load warning is present
with the same failure text on both qualified 6.12.26 boots and both 6.12.27
boots. It predates this two-commit ACK delta; Wi-Fi, cellular data, DNS, and
handoff validation all passed.

The first 6.12.27 evidence capture ran beyond 120 seconds and therefore
included the downstream Oplus hung-task enhancer's periodic reports for
`adci_thread`, `zram_comp`, `hfi_core_dbg_cl`, and `osml_monitor`. Their stacks
showed the existing OEM worker threads waiting in their normal completion,
listener, or polling paths. They did not produce a kernel hung-task timeout,
watchdog bite, subsystem restart, functional failure, or pstore record. The
qualified 6.12.26 comparison captures ended at 42 and 112 seconds, before the
same periodic reporting point, so this observation is documented rather than
claimed as a byte-for-byte baseline comparison.

Existing early Oplus/Qualcomm warning classes include PMIC arbiter,
`proc_register`, GIC selection, IRQ-enable, UFS query-attribute, Bluetooth bind,
and touch `frame_put` messages. The same classes occur in the qualified 6.12.26
evidence across its two boots. No new point-release-specific fatal warning
class was identified.

Complete first- and second-boot `dmesg`, `logcat`, `/proc/modules`, radio,
network, biometric, deep-idle, backup, and read-back evidence is retained under
the ignored local directory above and is not committed.

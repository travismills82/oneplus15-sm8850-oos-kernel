## Linux stable replay

The official Linux stable interval from v6.12.81 through the peeled v6.12.89
commit `51eba7e6c91ada319dc9ebee3f88e4472b5efbc4` contains 703 commits.  The
replay audit is recorded in
`oos1609400-linux-6.12.81-to-6.12.89-commits.tsv`.

| Result | Count |
|---|---:|
| Imported as individual commits | 695 |
| Equivalent fix already present | 8 |
| Unresolved or aggregate/squashed imports | 0 |

Original stable authorship, subjects, dependency order, and source SHAs are
recorded per row.  The eight omitted duplicates are individually identified
in the audit and point to their pre-existing equivalent commits.

The replay required KMI5 reconciliation around `struct device`, fwnode flags,
and per-task stack randomization.  The completed Android 6.12.89 history was
used for the authoritative driver-core, fwnode, and randomize-kstack reverts.
The stable `user_dumpable` behavior is retained through internal accessors
while the original raw `u64 __kabi_reserved3` task-structure record remains
unchanged.  Final `struct task_struct` size, alignment, and member offsets are
byte-for-byte identical to the qualified 6.12.80/6.12.81 KMI5 oracle.

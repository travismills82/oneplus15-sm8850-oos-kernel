# Linux 6.12.52 to 6.12.53 KMI5 pre-scan

## Authoritative boundary

The official kernel.org tag `v6.12.52` peels to
`2b2cbdcede3878c424b7937c83660ec9e9a232a2`; `v6.12.53` peels to
`fcd03f7736b1fa2b2181a7306d14008aa36b66ed`.  The former is an ancestor of
the latter.  The interval contains 261 non-merge commits and zero merges.
Both endpoints naturally report their matching `SUBLEVEL` values.

The Android Common `6.12.53` merge, `970096412ba737d7ed592490e9aefc2a2e5a0f02`,
still declares `KMI_GENERATION=5`.  The later generation bump
`889d5333b9f1e133bf13badc4df829040b6120fb` is not its ancestor; it first
appears before the Android `6.12.54` merge.  This project nevertheless imports
the Linux stable interval directly and treats Android Common only as a repair
reference.

## Machine-readable inventory

`oos1610500-linux-6.12.52-to-6.12.53-commits.tsv` records every official
commit in order, original author and subject, changed files, subsystem,
preliminary ABI classification, baseline applicability, and action.

Initial results against the exact 6.12.52 common tree:

- 261 official commits inventoried;
- 247 patches apply directly to the baseline snapshot;
- two F2FS fixes are already present and must not be duplicated;
- 12 patches require ordered/context review;
- eight touch a public/module-visible header or export surface;
- 16 touch a boot/FBE-sensitive implementation path;
- no merge commit is present in the Linux stable interval.

The 12 context results are not classified as defects.  The applicability scan
tests every patch independently against the starting snapshot; later patches
may depend on earlier commits in the same interval.  Final status is updated
after ordered replay and review.

## Known high-risk DM pair

The final portion of the interval contains:

- `e258ecf0c2a8f621ca2d08bbce6ceff4820cc762`, adding explicit DM request-queue
  stopped-state tracking; and
- `19ca4528666990be376ac3eb6fe667b03db5324d`, making suspend steps conditional
  on a loaded map.

Earlier OnePlus 15 experiments physically passed TWRP FBE and Android user0
only after both changes were excluded.  That evidence does not authorize
silently dropping stable fixes.  The official commits will remain individual
history entries, while any OOS reconciliation must be a separate downstream
commit that preserves the upstream race/NULL-dereference protection and passes
the physical FBE oracle.

## Gates before flash

Ordered replay, source review, build, strict KMI, symtypes, enforced common
ABI, Canoe ABI, final configuration comparison, stock-module provider/import
replay, private-layout analysis, signing validation, and boot-container
validation are mandatory.  ABI additions are not authorization to update the
reference.  No device write is permitted while either DM behavior or any
module/ABI gate remains unresolved.
